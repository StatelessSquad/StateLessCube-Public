How it plays

You already know the rules. That is the point.

  1. Swipe to move

    Drag the board in any direction. Matching icons merge and a new one drops in.

  2. Chain the merges

    Merge again straight away to build a combo — the streak is worth chasing.

  3. Climb to the Cube

    Every point moves you up the ladder. Reach the StateLess-Cube and keep going.
