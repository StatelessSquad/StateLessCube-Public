Everything that makes it hard to put down

Small details, tuned until every tap feels like it should pay off. Nothing is bolted on — the whole game is one idea: make the next merge feel better than the last.

🔥

Combo streaks

Merge on consecutive moves and the streak builds. The sound climbs a pentatonic scale, sparks fly, and the board celebrates you.

🥇

1000 levels

A ladder from Newbie to Stateless, with ranks, a progress ring and a new goal every few minutes.

🥇

Charged boosters

Every merge charges the meter. Fill it and you earn a hammer, a shuffle or a ×2 streak.

🎨

Nine worlds, one game

Robots & AI, ABC, Hebrew, Animals, Space, Fruits, Candy, Prism and Pixel — each with its own duotone palette, its own icons and its own mood. The rules never change; the feeling always does.

🔊

Sound you can feel

Every sound is synthesised live — no audio files, no wait, and it reacts to what you do.

📱

Made for thumbs

Swipe, tap, undo. It fits the screen, respects the notch and stays smooth on a phone.

✋

One-hand friendly

Undo is always one tap away, and the layout never moves under your thumb.

🧠

Real merge rules

No timers on your score, no energy, no tricks. The numbers add up the way they always did — the fun comes from how it feels, not from what it does to you.

🌗

Day & night

A warm candy light mode and a deep neon dark mode, following your phone by default and one tap away when you want it otherwise.

Everything is rendered on-device. No account, no ads, no analytics, no network calls — your score stays on your phone.

Pick your poison

Nine themes, each with its own colour ramp and its own set of icons. Change the mood of the whole game in one tap.
